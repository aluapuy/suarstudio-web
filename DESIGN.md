# Suar Studio — Design Direction (`DESIGN.md`)

> **Design Read:**
> Reading this as: **Sacred Editorial Ministry Studio Landing Page** for **Senior Pastors, Church Boards (Majelis), and Multimedia Leads**, in a **Warm Parchment & Archival Print** visual language, dial **ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2**.

## 1. Identity & Personality
- **Brand:** Suar Studio (`@suarstudio.co`)
- **Positioning:** A human-stewarded, AI-accelerated digital discipleship studio for Indonesian and diaspora churches. Never a generic self-serve clipping bot.
- **Aesthetic Soul:** Sacred Editorial Print meets Warm Studio Precision (inspired by Kinfolk print journals, Monocle editorial layouts, and archival church bulletins).

## 2. Active Color Palette (`R-01`, `R-25`, `R-29`)
- **Ground Surfaces (Neutrals):**
  - `Alabaster (#F6F4EE)`: Primary warm paper ground plane.
  - `Warm Sand (#EAE6DF)`: Secondary card and callout surface.
  - `Sand Border (#DCD6CC)`: Crisp architectural hairline borders (`1px`).
  - `Obsidian (#161615)` & `Charcoal (#1C1B19)`: Deep ink for primary typography and the dark Studio Showcase stage (`16.8:1` contrast on Alabaster).
  - `Slate (#534F49)`: Secondary body ink (`7.05:1` contrast on Alabaster, `6.24:1` on Warm Sand — passes WCAG AA normal text).
- **Core Brand Accents (2 Core + 1 Primary Action Accent):**
  - `Terracotta (#9E4424)` (Primary Action Accent): Reserved for primary CTAs and key editorial callouts (`5.58:1` contrast on Alabaster, `100%` WCAG AA compliant).
  - `Cypress (#264D3A)` (Verification & Pastoral Trust): Used strictly for Monday 09:00 WhatsApp sign-off badges, verbatim guarantees, and approved states (`8.45:1` contrast on Alabaster).
  - `Warm Brass (#C59B27)` (Editorial Rule & Active Focus): Used for serif italic underlines, active borders, and keyboard focus rings (`:focus-visible`).

## 3. Typography (`R-06`, `R-31`)
- **Display & Headings (`Instrument Serif`):** Brings the warmth and reverence of a printed Sunday bulletin and devotional book to digital screens.
- **Body & UI Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** High-x-height humanist sans-serif engineered for effortless reading in both Indonesian and English across mobile viewports.
- **Timestamps & Verbatim Codes (`JetBrains Mono`):** Used strictly for real sermon timecodes (`[24:15 - 25:10]`), durations (`[00:54]`), and schedule markers (`SENIN 09:00`). Never used for large headings.

## 4. Liveliness Dials (`Part 3`)
- **Dial:** `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2`
- **ENERGY 2 (Balanced Warmth):** Confident editorial serif scale paired with tactile phone mockups.
- **RHYTHM 3 (Varied Section Compositions):**
  1. *Hero:* 2-column asymmetric split with interactive 3D concave phone cylinder + Monday 09:00 approval card.
  2. *Why Suar (PainPoints):* 3-column diagnostic comparison (Manual NLE vs. Instant Clippers vs. Suar Standard) + 3 editorial benefit columns.
  3. *The Weekly Rhythm (SopTimeline):* Horizontal/2-column split pairing the 3-step Monday schedule with a live interactive WhatsApp approval simulator.
  4. *Weekly Ecosystem (EcosystemTabs):* High-contrast Obsidian dark-room studio stage with interactive category tabs and 3-phone visual proofs.
  5. *Near-Future Church Automation Roadmap (Roadmap):* Asymmetric editorial showcase explicitly badged as *"Segera Hadir · Dalam Pengembangan Aktif"* (*"Coming Very Soon · In Active Development"*), separating live clipping services from upcoming church automations.
  6. *Pricing & Founding Partner Pass (Pricing):* 3 transparent monthly retainers anchored by a perforated physical-style *"Founding Partner Trial Pass #0001"* voucher.
- **MOTION 2 (Purposeful Transitions):** Direct pointer-drag physics on the 3D phone cylinder, tab layout transitions, and interactive WhatsApp state progression. Zero endless un-triggered loops (`animate-ping` / infinite waveform bounce removed).

## 5. One-Line Purpose Log (`R-31`)
- **Warm Alabaster paper background:** Evokes printed church devotionals and avoids sterile tech-SaaS white or cold dark-mode defaults.
- **3D Concave Phone Cylinder in Hero:** Lets pastors and media leads physically swipe through real Ibadah Raya, Youth, and Kids smartphone outputs on mobile and desktop.
- **Interactive WhatsApp Demo:** Proves in 3 seconds that Monday 09:00 sign-off requires only a simple `"OK"` reply on WhatsApp, with zero new software dashboards to learn.
- **Dark Obsidian background on EcosystemTabs only:** Creates a deliberate studio screening-room contrast break (`RHYTHM 3`) so the illustrated phone screens pop with maximum clarity.
- **Explicit "Segera Hadir / Coming Very Soon" badges on Roadmap:** Maintains 100% honesty (`R-38`) that church-wide automations (RAG archive, newcomer WA care, multilingual bulletin, book compilation) are in active development for partner churches rather than pretending they are already live off-the-shelf products today.
